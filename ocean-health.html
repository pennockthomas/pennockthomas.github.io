The tour

Welcome

An interactive map of Ocean Health innovations: nature-based solutions that can be added to marine projects, and where they fit.

2 / 4

Explore the island

Tap a glowing ring to open a project area and see which innovations belong there. Drag to rotate the view, pinch or scroll to zoom.

3 / 4

Look underwater

Some areas have a dive button. It takes the camera below the waterline to show what happens on the seabed.

4 / 4

Innovations

Every solution in one list: when to use it in a tender, the evidence behind it, and who to contact.

Under the hood

A 3D scene is the part Flutter can’t do alone.

1 / 3

Flutter shell, Three.js scene

The interface and navigation are Flutter. The 3D island is Three.js, shown in a web view on iOS and macOS and in an iframe on the web. The land itself is hexagon tiles over a heightmap.

2 / 3

Water that moves like water

The sea is drawn with Gerstner waves: several moving swells added together in a shader, so it has a believable rhythm without a heavy simulation.

3 / 3

Wind farm and models

The island carries a wind farm and a dredger, a real 3D model, so the work looks like the work it stands for.

What I learned

And what is still in progress.

1 / 2
